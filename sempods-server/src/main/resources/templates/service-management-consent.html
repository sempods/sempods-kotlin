This service manages your pod

It calls itself Owner Console. Any service can choose that name.

It acts without you, until you remove it. It can:

  • see the name of every context on your pod,
  • create contexts,
  • delete contexts, with everything in them,
  • see the grants people, apps and services hold on each context, and withdraw them.

Withdrawing grants does not change who can read a public context: anyone can.

It reads none of your data. It loses this when you remove it or no longer own the pod.

If you did not just set up this service, or the identifier is not the one it showed you, cancel.
